# The Letter That Arrived Too Late

A six-word story about a grandmother's final letter reaching home
after she is gone — a lifetime of waiting, and a goodbye that can
never be answered. The last two words carry the turn: the letter
arrives, but "after her" means the person who wrote it is already
beyond reply. Everything unsaid — who waited, what the letter held,
whether it was ever opened — is left for the reader.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
